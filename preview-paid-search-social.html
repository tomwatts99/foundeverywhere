Paid Search & Social

Show up from day one

While your organic and AI visibility builds, paid campaigns across Google, Meta, and LinkedIn put you in front of the right audience immediately.

The problem

Organic visibility takes time. You need leads now.

SEO and AI visibility are long-term investments that compound over time. But most businesses need enquiries coming in while that foundation is being built.

Paid search and social fills the gap - putting you in front of people actively searching for what you offer, and in front of audiences who don't know you exist yet but should.

Common questions

What people ask about paid search and social

What budget do I need?

It depends on your industry and goals. We will advise on realistic budgets for your sector and won't take on campaigns where the budget isn't sufficient to get meaningful results.

Do you manage campaigns or just set them up?

We manage campaigns on an ongoing basis - setup and walk away isn't something we offer. Paid campaigns need continuous optimisation to perform.

Which platform should I start with?

For most local businesses, Google Ads first. If you're B2B, LinkedIn. If you're selling visually or need to build awareness, Meta. We will advise on the right starting point for your specific situation.

Can paid ads work alongside SEO?

Yes - and they work better together. Paid fills the gap while organic builds. Retargeting campaigns also convert people who found you organically but didn't enquire first time.

How do you measure success?

We agree on the right metrics upfront - usually cost per lead or cost per acquisition rather than clicks or impressions. Vanity metrics don't pay your bills.

How do we get started?

Get in touch and tell us about your business and your goals. We will review your current situation and tell you honestly whether paid search is the right move right now.
